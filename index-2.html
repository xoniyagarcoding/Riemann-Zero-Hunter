Riemann Zero Hunter

A game built on the Riemann Hypothesis, powered by the real zeta function

Z(t) = 2 Σ cos(θ(t) − t·ln n) / √n + R(t)
  • The curve is Hardy's Z function: the value of ζ(½ + it) along the critical line, computed live with the Riemann–Siegel formula.
  • Every time the curve crosses the axis, that is a non-trivial zero of the zeta function.
  • The orb rides the curve. Tap exactly as it crosses the axis (button, screen tap or Space).
  • Follow the shrinking ring. Miss a zero or tap too early and you lose a life.
